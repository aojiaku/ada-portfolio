Queue Health Monitor

Leaders had plenty of data, but no single place to see how any team was actually doing. Case volume, on-time rates, quality scores, and speed all lived in different tools, so a 70-slide weekly meeting could explain what happened but rarely why. I built a single view that puts every team on the same three simple measures: how much work is coming in, how fast it's handled, and how well it's done.

Click a team card to see its 8-week trend and what's actually going on behind it.
