Queue Health Monitor

My team had to explain why 30-plus metrics moved across 70-plus workflows, every week. The context lived in a dozen places: launches, experiments, bugs, known issues, longer trends. So we rebuilt the root cause by hand in a shared spreadsheet, and it forgot everything we'd learned the week before. I built one view that puts every metric next to its context, organized under three questions leaders actually ask: do we have enough people, are we fast enough, and are we getting it right? Then I added AI scaffolding that drafts the first-pass root cause from the documents my team already tracked, so the team validates instead of rewriting.
