# About Me

I'm Ada Johnson, a Product Strategy and Operations leader. I build the systems that help organizations turn messy signal into clear decisions.

Right now I lead Trust and Safety Product Operations at Airbnb, where my team keeps fraud and safety workflows running well, explains why performance is moving the way it is, and makes sure what we learn actually drives decisions.

Before Airbnb, I spent seven years at Google across IT Support, Google One, and the Google Store, and before that, Target and Best Buy. Twenty years total, all in the same throughline: get close to the signal a company isn't using yet, then build the structure to turn it into action.

A few things about how I work:
- I care more about clarity than complexity. If something can be said simply, I'll say it simply.
- I'm most useful in ambiguous situations. Give me a messy problem and not enough process, and I'll build the process.
- Lately I've gone hands-on with AI: using it to synthesize feedback, spot patterns, and automate work that used to take hours. I want to keep going deeper on that, finding where AI creates real leverage and working with product and engineering to make it scalable.
