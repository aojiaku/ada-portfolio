The Problem Library

Every day, frontline employees were spotting real problems: bugs, gaps, patterns worth flagging. But that feedback was scattered across emails, chats, and forms, with no way to tell a problem that kept coming up from a one-time complaint. I saw that gap and built the system to close it, turning scattered signal into a single library leaders can act on. You can also ask it questions directly, instead of digging through a spreadsheet.
