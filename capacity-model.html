Capacity Model

My team juggled four different kinds of work at the same time: regular reporting meetings, big strategic projects, everyday casework, and last-minute fires to put out. I could explain any single project, but not our total workload or where the real tradeoffs were. I built a model that connects team size and workload to what's actually needed in each area, so a conversation about priorities can start with real numbers instead of a gut feeling.

Drag any slider below and watch the numbers update.

Quick note: the category names and hours below are made up. The idea behind it, team size and hours compared to what each area actually needs, matches a real planning tool I built and use.
